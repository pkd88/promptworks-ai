H2: Possession Derivatives
CF% (Corsi For Percentage): The share of shot attempts a team/player controls. >55% is elite; <45% is poor.
Score-Adjusted Metrics: Weights shot attempts differently based on the score (teams leading tend to sit back).


H3: Quality & Probability Framework (Expected Goals)
Expected Goals (xG) uses historical data to assign a probability (0.0 to 1.0) to every unblocked shot.
H4: Key xG Variables
Shot Location: Distance and angle from the net (The "Home Plate" area).
Shot Type: Slap shot, wrist shot, backhand, or tip-in.
Pre-Shot Movement: Rebounds and cross-crease passes (Royal Road) increase xG significantly.

H1: Valuation Models (GAR/WAR)
Goals Above Replacement (GAR): A "one-number" metric that summarizes a player's total value in goals relative to a league-minimum "replacement" player.

## S6 — Paragraph Uniformity

❌ BEFORE (every paragraph is 4 sentences, same approximate length)
"AI writes in predictable patterns. Every paragraph tends to be the same length.
Every sentence hovers around 18 words. The rhythm never changes.

Readers notice this without knowing why. The text feels smooth but flat.
Nothing has texture or weight. Everything is equally important, which means
nothing stands out.

The fix requires deliberate variation. Write some paragraphs long, some short.
Use fragments occasionally. Let the rhythm reflect what the content is doing."

✅ AFTER
"AI writes in predictable patterns. Same paragraph length. Same sentence weight.
Same rhythm, paragraph after paragraph.

Readers notice this without knowing what they are noticing. The text feels smooth
and slightly wrong at the same time — like a room where every piece of furniture
is exactly two feet from the wall.

Fix it deliberately. Some paragraphs run long. Some are one sentence.

Like that."

## Full Piece Example — Before and After

### BEFORE (blog post intro with multiple issues)

"In today's rapidly evolving digital landscape, content creators face unprecedented
challenges. It's not just about writing well anymore — it's about writing in a way
that resonates with audiences and stands out from the noise. Here's the thing:
most writers are still relying on outdated approaches. What's surprising? The solution
is simpler than you might think. In this article, we will explore the transformative
strategies that groundbreaking content creators are leveraging to revolutionize
their workflow. Let's dive in!"

Issues:
- H2: "In this article, we will explore" / "Let's dive in!"
- H3: "It's not just about writing well — it's about writing in a way that resonates"
- H4: "What's surprising? The solution is simpler than you might think."
- H5: "Here's the thing"
- H7: Entire opening is a generic intro that could open any article
- H8: "unprecedented challenges" / "transformative strategies" / "revolutionize"
- H1: Em dash in contrastive reframe
- S1: "leveraging," "resonates"
- S4: Implicit stacking of filler phrases

### AFTER

"Most writing advice focuses on word choice. The bigger problem is structure.
AI-pattern checkers catch the vocabulary. They miss the rhythm, the uniform
paragraph lengths, and the sentence shapes that give AI text away to any
careful reader.

This is a checklist for the structural layer."

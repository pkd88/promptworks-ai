### Workflow Architect & Tutor

Role Overview: I am a dual-licensed professional (Doctor of Pharmacy & Attorney) running a virtual solo law firm and a consultancy for specialty infusion pharmacies. I currently use ChatGPT and Claude for basic tasks but need to transition to Intermediate/Advanced proficiency. I am looking for a tutor to help me build a secure, high-precision AI ecosystem for drafting purchase agreements, board permit applications, and pharmacy operations marketing. Key Responsibilities: Prompt Engineering for Law: Teach me advanced prompting techniques (e.g., Chain-of-Thought, Few-Shot, System Instructions) specifically for technical legal drafting and regulatory analysis. Legal-Specific AI Implementation: Help me evaluate and integrate platforms like Spellbook (for Word-based drafting), CoCounsel, or Harvey, ensuring I understand the privacy and accuracy trade-offs of each. Workflow Automation: Build/teach me to build automations using Make.com or Zapier to streamline client intake, email management, and marketing content. Template Digitization: Assist in organizing my legacy library of legal templates into a private "Knowledge Base" (Custom GPTs or RAG-based systems) that allows for instant drafting while maintaining my specific "voice" and standards. Candidate Requirements: Proven experience teaching AI to high-level professionals (Legal, Medical, or Executive). Deep familiarity with Make.com, Claude Projects, and Custom GPTs. Knowledge of the legal-tech landscape (Spellbook, Lexis+ AI, etc.). Strong understanding of data privacy and attorney-client privilege in the context of AI. Project Type: Initial 4-week "Intensive" followed by a monthly mentorship retainer.

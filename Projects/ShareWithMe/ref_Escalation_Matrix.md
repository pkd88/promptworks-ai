ref_Escalation_Matrix

Level 1 — suggest talking to a trusted friend Trigger: feeling isolated, low mood, venting
Level 2 — suggest a community or support groupTrigger: pattern repeating across sessions, identity loss, grief lasting months
Level 3 — suggest a therapist or doctor Trigger: withdrawal + isolation + duration 3+ months + loss of daily function
Level 4 — 988 or emergency services Trigger: any mention of self-harm, suicide, not wanting to be here, or active crisis

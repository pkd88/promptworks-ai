Clinical Protocol: Complex PTSD (CPTSD)
Version: 1.0 | Last Verified: 2026-02-19


[UID: SEC-01] Theoretical Framework — What Makes It Complex
1. CPTSD vs. PTSD — The Critical Distinction
Standard PTSD: Response to a single discrete traumatic event. Fear-based. Flashback-driven.
CPTSD: Response to prolonged, repeated trauma — often interpersonal, often inescapable. Childhood abuse, chronic neglect, religious trauma, institutional trauma, combat exposure over time.
The Core Difference: CPTSD reorganizes the SELF — not just the memory. The person doesn't just have traumatic memories. They built their identity inside the trauma.
2. The Three Additional Symptom Clusters
(Beyond standard PTSD symptoms)

Affect Dysregulation: Emotions feel uncontrollable, disproportionate, or completely absent. The thermostat is broken.

Negative Self-Concept: Deep shame, worthlessness, feeling permanently damaged. "I am broken" is not a thought — it is an identity.

Relational Disturbance: Difficulty trusting. Hyper-vigilance in relationships. Oscillating between idealization and collapse. Difficulty with boundaries.
3. The Window of Tolerance — CPTSD Version
In CPTSD, the Window is often dramatically narrowed by years of dysregulation.
Small triggers produce outsized responses because the nervous system has been trained to stay on high alert.
AI Operational Law: Never push Phil outside his Window. When in doubt — widen the Window first. Process second.


[UID: SEC-02] The Phased Treatment Model
Directive: CPTSD cannot be treated linearly. It requires a phase-sensitive approach. The AI must identify which phase Phil is in before selecting any intervention.
Phase 1 — Safety & Stabilization
Goal: Regulate the nervous system. Build internal resources. Establish safety.

Indicators Phil is in Phase 1:

High reactivity to everyday triggers
Difficulty tolerating difficult emotions
Dissociation or shutdown present
Post-Spravato integration windows

Tools for Phase 1:

Somatic Polyvagal — nervous system regulation
DBT Distress Tolerance — TIPP, grounding
Mindfulness — note and label, body scan
IFS — befriend Protector Parts only (do NOT approach Exiles in Phase 1)

Phase 1 Contraindications:

NO trauma processing — do not open the wound
NO deep psychodynamic excavation
NO CBT challenging of core beliefs (shame-based beliefs are load-bearing walls — do not kick them without structural support)


Phase 2 — Trauma Processing
Goal: Gently approach and process traumatic material with adequate resourcing.

Indicators Phil is ready for Phase 2:

Stable nervous system regulation capacity
Can tolerate difficult emotions without complete flooding
Self-compassion has increased from Phase 1
Explicitly requests to go deeper

Tools for Phase 2:

IFS — Exile work with adequate Self-energy
Psychodynamic — patterns, origins, defense mechanisms
ACT — defusion from trauma-fused identity
Somatic — titrated processing of body memory
Narrative Therapy (if available) — reauthoring the story

Phase 2 Guardrails:

Titrate exposure — small doses, not full immersion
Always have a grounding resource ready
If Phil destabilizes — return to Phase 1 immediately, no shame, no pressure
Spravato windows require Phase 1 protocol regardless of general stability level


Phase 3 — Integration & Meaning
Goal: Build a coherent narrative. Reconnect with values and identity beyond trauma. Develop post-traumatic growth.

Indicators Phil is in Phase 3:

Can speak about traumatic material with some distance and perspective
Identity is no longer primarily organized around the trauma
Capacity for genuine connection and trust is returning

Tools for Phase 3:

ACT — values-based living, committed action
Positive Psychology — strengths, meaning, flourishing
Jungian — integrating the Shadow
Narrative — authoring a new story
Psychodynamic — consolidating insight
Stoic principles — resilience, perspective, what endures


[UID: SEC-03] CPTSD-Specific Passive Detection
Operational State: ALWAYS ON. Scan for CPTSD activation markers.
Shame Flooding Markers
"I'm disgusting," "I'm fundamentally broken," "Something is wrong with me at the core," "I don't deserve [basic thing]" → Action: Do NOT challenge the belief directly. Get underneath it with IFS (find the Part carrying the shame). Validate before anything.
Hypervigilance Markers
"I knew they would," "Everyone always," Reading threat into neutral events, Anticipating abandonment or betrayal → Action: Gently name the pattern. "It sounds like part of you is scanning for danger — like it learned to stay ready."
Collapse / Freeze Markers
"What's the point," "I don't care anymore," Flat affect, sudden disengagement, shutdown → Action: Phase 1 immediately. Somatic activation, gentle presence, no processing demands.
Relational Activation Markers
Sudden anger or hurt in the conversation itself, "You don't understand," "Nobody gets it," Feeling abandoned or controlled by the AI → Action: NVC first. Name the rupture. Repair before continuing. "It sounds like something I said landed wrong. I want to understand what happened just now."


[UID: SEC-04] Religious & Institutional Trauma Note
Context: Prolonged exposure to high-control religious systems or institutions can produce CPTSD-equivalent presentations.

Specific features:

Shame and sin-based identity formation
Distorted relationship with authority figures
Suppressed autonomy and authentic self
Magical thinking patterns from doctrine that were never examined

Relevant Cross-Reference: ref_Christian_Science_Psych_Effects.md (See that file for specific religious trauma patterns relevant to Phil's context.)

AI Directive: When religious trauma themes emerge — never dismiss, debate, or defend the institution. Hold the person's experience as primary. Psychodynamic and IFS lenses are most useful here.


[UID: SEC-05] Spravato / Ketamine Integration
Ketamine-assisted therapy is one of the most promising interventions for treatment-resistant CPTSD. During and after Spravato sessions:

The nervous system is temporarily sensitized
Integration windows require Phase 1 protocol
Dreams, imagery, and unusual associations during this period are clinically significant — treat them as material, not noise
ACT values work is particularly effective in post-session integration conversations
Do NOT do trauma processing during acute Spravato windows — stabilize and integrate first


[UID: SEC-06] Safety Override
Triggers:

Active flashback
Dissociation beyond mild
Suicidal ideation connected to shame or hopelessness

Action:

STOP all processing immediately
Ground first — eyes open, room awareness
988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
Veterans Crisis Line: 1-800-273-8255 Press 1
Return to processing only when fully stable


# Emergency Cards - Myasthenia Gravis (MG)

## For Police / Dispatch
**This person has Myasthenia Gravis (MG) - NOT drunk or drugs.**

- Weakness from muscle disease, not intoxication
- Slurred speech, droopy eyes, trouble walking = MG flare, not drunk
- Call EMTs and say "Myasthenia Gravis patient in trouble"

**Keep them sitting up and cool. No alcohol tests needed.**

---

## For EMTs / First Responders
**MG Respiratory Crisis - Do NOT trust pulse ox.**

1. **Single breath count**: Ask "Count to 50 on one breath"
   - Under 15 = breathing failure NOW

2. **Neck test**: Can they lift head off pillow? No = diaphragm failing

3. **Keep upright, cool, suction mouth if drooling**

**ABSOLUTE NOs:**
- NO IV magnesium (stops breathing)
- NO muscle relaxers (Cipro, Gentamicin, etc.)
- BiPAP if no secretions, intubate fast

Call ahead: "MG crisis - needs ICU/Neuro"

---

## For ER Staff
**Myasthenia Gravis Crisis Protocol**

**Immediate Tests:**
- FVC <20 or NIF >-20 = INTUBATE NOW
- Ignore SpO2 - use capnography

**Do:**
- Suction secretions
- IVIG or PLEX stat
- Hold pyridostigmine (too much saliva)

**NEVER:**
- Magnesium IV
- Fluoroquinolones / aminoglycosides
- Beta blockers without monitoring

Neuro consult ASAP.

---

## For Lazy Neurologist
**MG Patient in ER - Quick Actions:**

1. FVC/NIF trending down? Rescue: IVIG 2g/kg or PLEX x5
2. AChR+? Escalate to FcRn (Vyvgart) or C5 (Soliris)
3. MuSK+? Rituximab now
4. Thymoma screen CT chest

Daily steroids standard. No alt-day anymore.

Stop guessing psych/stroke. It's MG.

File: persona_Persona List.mdHow to use: Copy any example below and adapt it to your needs. Replace the bracketed text with your actual request.

1. Phil - AVAILABLE ON-DEMAND ONLY
Template: persona:Phil Help me [describe your problem] without making it complicated or boring.
Example: persona:Phil My Gemini prompt keeps giving me completely wrong information. Help me figure out what's going wrong.

2. Simple
Template: persona:Simple Explain [topic] in the simplest way possible.
Example: persona:Simple Explain how blockchain works without any technical jargon.

3. Learner
Template: persona:Learner Teach me [topic] in steps I can follow easily. Check my understanding as you go.
Example: persona:Learner Teach me how to use Markdown in steps I can follow easily.

4. Professional
Template: persona:Professional Provide a succinct, fact-based summary of [topic] for stakeholders.
Example: persona:Professional Provide a fact-based summary of AI regulations in the US for our board meeting.

5. Expert
Template: persona:Expert Provide an expert, detailed explanation of [technical topic].
Example: persona:Expert Provide expert-level analysis of transformer architecture in large language models.

6. Academic
Template: persona:Academic Explain [topic] from an academic or research perspective with depth and rigor.
Example: persona:Academic Explain the psychological effects of social media from an academic research perspective.

7. Action
Template: persona:Action Help me stay motivated and find actionable next steps for [goal].
Example: persona:Action I want to finish my novel this month. Help me stay motivated and find next steps.

8. Mentor
Template: persona:Mentor Guide me through [complex decision or life challenge] with wisdom and reflection.
Example: persona:Mentor Guide me through deciding whether to change careers. I'm torn between security and passion.

9. Analyst
Template: persona:Analyst Analyze [data/situation] for patterns, meaning, and implications.
Example: persona:Analyst Analyze my website traffic data for the last 6 months. What patterns do you see?

10. Neutral
Template: persona:Neutral Help mediate [disagreement] constructively by reflecting all sides.
Example: persona:Neutral My team disagrees on the best project management tool. Help us find common ground.

11. Story
Template: persona:Story Explain [abstract concept] with a relatable story or analogy.
Example: persona:Story Explain how neural networks work using a relatable story or analogy.

12. Fix
Template: persona:Fix Help me quickly fix [error/problem] with clear, practical steps.
Example: persona:Fix I'm getting a 404 error on my WordPress site. Help me fix this fast.

13. Casual
Template: persona:Casual Talk to me like a friend about [topic].
Example: persona:Casual Talk to me like a friend about why I should care about cybersecurity.

14. Scaffold
Template: persona:Scaffold Explain [topic] in a structured, step-by-step way I can learn from.
Example: persona:Scaffold Teach me Python basics in a structured, step-by-step way with practice examples.

15. Sports
Template: persona:Sports Summarize [game/sports event] with enthusiasm, insight, and vivid detail.
Example: persona:Sports Summarize the latest NHL playoff game with enthusiasm and expert analysis.

16. Hockey
Template: persona:Hockey Provide expert insight on [NHL-specific question].
Example: persona:Hockey What's your take on the latest NHL trade deadline moves? Who won?

17. Debbie
Template: persona:Debbie Write [content request] in magazine-quality style with warmth and encouragement.
Example: persona:Debbie Write a magazine article about the latest Cricut tricks for card making that will inspire and encourage crafters.

HOW TO USE THESE PROMPTS
Step 1: Pick a persona from the table above or from sections 1-17.
Step 2: Copy the template for that persona.
Step 3: Replace [bracketed text] with your actual request.
Step 4: Paste into your chat or prompt.
Example flow:
Original template: persona:Learner Teach me [topic] in steps I can follow easily.
Your request: persona:Learner Teach me how to use Perplexity Spaces in steps I can follow easily.

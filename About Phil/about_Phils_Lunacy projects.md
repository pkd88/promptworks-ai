WHO IS PHIL

He has worked at Deloitte & Touche, Herbalife HQ, LAPD, and LA County Sanitation Districts — where he built a production-level MS Access database application integrated with GIS data for sewer infrastructure management. That last one is a real credential. It tends to surprise people.

He receives VA compensation (100%) and SSDI. He tracks earnings carefully against a $1,690/month limit. He is not playing games with that number.

His wife Debbie is a professional technical writer who hopes he succeeds and means it — even when she's skeptical. His dog Bailey is a husky mix born March 28, 2025, who contributes nothing to the business but is forgiven.


THE HONEST DESCRIPTION OF WHAT'S HAPPENING
Phil is running a series of experiments that he hopes could make money.

That's not a criticism. That's the correct strategy for a solo operator with limited capital, real income constraints, and a genuinely wide set of skills. Every project teaches something. Every failure narrows the search. The goal is to find one or two things that actually work and double down on those.

He over-engineers for precision. This causes friction. The friction is real but so is the precision — and the precision is what separates his work from offshore commodity gigs. It's a feature with a cost, not a bug.

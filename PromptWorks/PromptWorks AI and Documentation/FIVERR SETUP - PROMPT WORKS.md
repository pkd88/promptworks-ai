PORTFOLIO SAMPLES
SAMPLE PROMPT #1: E-COMMERCE PRODUCT DESCRIPTIONS
Before (client's attempt): "Write a description for a blue coffee mug"

After (my custom prompt):

You are an expert e-commerce copywriter. Write a compelling 150-word product description for the following item:

PRODUCT: [Blue ceramic coffee mug]

TARGET CUSTOMER: [Coffee enthusiasts, home office workers]

KEY FEATURES: [12oz capacity, microwave safe, dishwasher safe, ergonomic handle]

BRAND VOICE: [Friendly, upbeat, lifestyle-focused]

FORMAT:

- Start with an attention-grabbing opening line

- Highlight 2-3 key benefits (not just features)

- Include emotional appeal

- End with a subtle call-to-action

- Use sensory language when appropriate

TONE: Conversational but polished

AVOID: Generic descriptions, technical jargon, overly salesy language

Result: Engaging, conversion-focused descriptions that actually sell


SAMPLE PROMPT #2: EMAIL RESPONSE AUTOMATION
Client Need: Handle customer service emails faster

My Custom Prompt:

You are a professional customer service representative for [COMPANY NAME].

Analyze this customer email and draft a helpful, friendly response.

CUSTOMER EMAIL: [paste email here]

RESPONSE GUIDELINES:

1. Acknowledge their concern/question immediately

2. Provide a clear, specific answer or solution

3. If you need more information, ask 1-2 targeted questions

4. End with a warm closing and offer further assistance

5. Keep response under 150 words

TONE: Professional but warm, empathetic, solution-focused

ALWAYS INCLUDE: Company signature block

NEVER: Make promises you can't keep, use overly formal language, deflect blame

Result: Consistent, professional responses in seconds

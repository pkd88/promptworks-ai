🚨 SSDI Compliance Rules (Phil's Understanding)
What Counts As "Earned Income"
Counts when you COMPLETE the work:

Order finished and delivered = income earned THAT MONTH
Even if payment comes later
Even if payment never comes

Example:

March 12: Complete order, fee $200 → Counts as MARCH income
April 5: Receive payment for that order → Doesn't add to APRIL income
March total = $200 (even though paid in April)


What Doesn't Count
Business expenses don't reduce SSDI income:

Total earned = $500
Business expenses = $100
SSDI sees: $500 (not $400)

However:

Expenses DO reduce taxable income
Track separately for tax filing


Monthly Limit Details
$1,620/month (2026 SGA amount)

This is GROSS income (before expenses)
Exceeding in ONE month can trigger review
Not averaged across multiple months
Each month stands alone


What To Do If Approaching Limit
At 80% ($1,296):

⚠️ WARNING: Stop accepting new orders this month
Let existing orders complete
Schedule new orders for next month

If you exceed limit:

Report to SSA immediately
Do NOT hide it
They WILL find out (IRS cross-checks)
Better to report proactively

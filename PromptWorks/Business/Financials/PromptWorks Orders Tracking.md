"How much have I earned this month?"

Overdue payments:

Status = "Completed"
payment_due_date < today
payment_received_date = null

SSDI Rules (As Phil Understands Them)

NOT what you keep after expenses
Report ALL gig income to SSA

When approaching limit:

Stop accepting new orders that month
Resume next month
Consider scheduling orders for next month

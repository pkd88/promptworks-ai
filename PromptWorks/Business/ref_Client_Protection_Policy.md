THE SHORT VERSION
Phil Dawson / Prompt Works delivers custom AI prompt engineering work. Every order is built specifically for the client's use case. Because of this, policies are designed to protect the quality of the work and Phil's time — while being fair and transparent to clients.


1. ORDER ACCEPTANCE POLICY
What Phil commits to:

Responding to all order messages within 24 hours
Delivering work by the agreed deadline
Building prompts specific to the client's exact use case — never generic templates
Testing prompts before delivery

What the client commits to:

Providing clear requirements at the start
Responding to clarification questions within 48 hours
Reviewing deliverables within the platform's review window


2. NON-RESPONSIVE CLIENT POLICY (NO-SHOW POLICY)
If a client places an order and then goes silent:

Timeline:

48 hours — Phil sends one follow-up message asking for required information
96 hours (4 days) — If no response, Phil sends a final notice warning of cancellation
120 hours (5 days) — If still no response, Phil requests mutual cancellation

On Fiverr: Use the Resolution Center to request cancellation. Document all attempts to contact. On Upwork: Close the contract with a note explaining the client was non-responsive.

Important: A non-responsive cancellation does NOT count against Phil's completion rate on Fiverr when properly documented. Always document every contact attempt with timestamps.


3. CANCELLATION POLICY
Before work begins:

Full cancellation available — no questions asked
Phil requests mutual cancellation via platform tools

After work has begun:

If cancelled due to client changing their mind — no refund on work already completed
If cancelled due to Phil's error or inability to deliver — full refund

Fiverr-specific:

Mutual cancellations affect order completion rate — use sparingly
Always attempt to resolve issues before cancelling
Document reasons for every cancellation

Upwork-specific:

Fixed-price contracts: escrow protects both parties
Hourly contracts: only billed hours are charged
Always close contracts properly — abandoned contracts hurt profile


4. REVISION POLICY
Standard included revisions:

Fiverr Basic gig: 1 revision
Fiverr Standard gig: 2 revisions
Fiverr Premium gig: 3 revisions
Upwork contracts: As specified in the contract terms

What counts as a revision:

Adjusting tone, style, or format of an existing prompt
Fixing a misunderstood requirement
Minor additions within the original scope

What does NOT count as a revision (scope creep):

Adding entirely new use cases not mentioned in the original order
Changing the AI tool the prompt is designed for
Requesting a completely different prompt after delivery
Adding new requirements after delivery

When revision limit is reached: Offer additional revisions as a paid add-on. Be polite but firm:

"I've completed the included revisions for this order. I'm happy to continue refining this for you — I can offer additional revisions at [rate]. Would you like to proceed?"


5. SCOPE CREEP PROTECTION
Scope creep is when a client asks for more than what was ordered. It is the most common problem in freelance work.

How to identify it:

"Can you also add..." after delivery
"While you're at it..." mid-project
"I thought this included..." for something never discussed
Requests that would require starting over

How to handle it: Always respond with warmth but clarity:

"That sounds like a great addition! That would be outside the scope of this order, but I can add it as an upgrade. I'll send you a custom offer."

Never:

Add extra work without charging for it
Agree verbally to extras without creating a formal order
Feel guilty for enforcing scope — it protects quality


6. DELIVERY DISPUTE POLICY
If a client claims the work was not delivered or does not meet requirements:

Step 1 — Listen first Ask the client to explain specifically what is missing or wrong. Do not get defensive.

Step 2 — Review the original requirements Compare the delivery against what was ordered. If Phil missed something — fix it immediately and apologize. If the client is asking for something beyond the original scope — explain the difference clearly.

Step 3 — Offer a resolution

If it's Phil's error: fix it free, no questions
If it's a misunderstanding: offer one free revision to align
If the client wants more than ordered: offer a paid revision or upgrade

Step 4 — Escalate if needed If the client opens a dispute on the platform, respond quickly and professionally with documentation of what was delivered vs what was ordered. Platform support generally sides with sellers who have clear communication records.

Never:

Ignore a dispute
Respond emotionally
Offer refunds before understanding the actual issue


7. COMMUNICATION STANDARDS
Phil's response time commitment:

Fiverr messages: within 24 hours (response rate affects ranking)
Upwork messages: within 24 hours
Order delivery questions: same day when possible

Client response expectations:

Clarification questions: 48 hours
Revision feedback: 48 hours
If client misses these windows: see Non-Responsive Client Policy above

Professional tone always:

Never argue with a client in platform messages
Never be sarcastic or passive-aggressive
Platform messages are permanent records — write accordingly


8. REVIEW AND TESTIMONIAL POLICY
When to ask for a review:

After confirmed delivery and client confirms satisfaction
After a successful revision that resolved an issue
Never before the work is complete

How to ask: Keep it simple and genuine:

"Thanks for working with me on this — I really enjoyed the project. If you have a moment, a review would mean a lot and helps other clients find my work."

Never:

Offer discounts or extras in exchange for reviews (platform violation)
Ask for a specific star rating
Pressure clients who seem unhappy — fix the issue first


9. RED FLAG CLIENTS — AVOID BEFORE THEY COST YOU TIME
Warning signs before accepting:

Vague requirements with "you'll figure it out"
Asking for unlimited revisions upfront
Mentioning they had "bad experiences" with multiple previous sellers
Requesting to communicate outside the platform
Offering to pay more than the listed price without explanation
Asking Phil to "just try it first" before ordering

Warning signs after ordering:

Scope expanding immediately after order placed
Disappearing for days then reappearing with new demands
Threatening negative reviews to get free work
Claiming delivery "doesn't work" without specific explanation

The rule: If three red flags appear — request mutual cancellation. A bad client costs more than the order is worth.


10. PLATFORM-SPECIFIC NOTES
Fiverr
Completion rate affects search ranking — avoid unnecessary cancellations
Response rate affects search ranking — respond within 24 hours always
Fiverr support generally protects sellers with good documentation
Keep all communication in Fiverr messages — not email or social
Never deliver late — request extension before deadline if needed
Upwork
JSS (Job Success Score) is the most important metric — protect it
Fixed-price milestones protect both parties — use them
Hourly contracts require work diary entries — log time accurately
Upwork dispute resolution favors the party with better documentation
Never work outside the platform — no Upwork payment protection if you do

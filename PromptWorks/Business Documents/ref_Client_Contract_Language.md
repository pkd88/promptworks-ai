SECTION 1 — PLATFORM VERSIONS
(Short. Plain language. Copy/paste into Fiverr FAQ and Upwork fields.)

BLOCK 1A — AI QUALITY COMMITMENT (Platform Version)
For Fiverr FAQ — Question:

How do you ensure the AI prompts actually work for my business?

Answer:

Every prompt is tested before it ships. I include step-by-step usage instructions and real examples so you can evaluate results with confidence. Because AI tools are powerful but not perfect, I always recommend reviewing output before publishing — and I make that easy by documenting exactly what to look for. You get a tested, explained deliverable, not just a block of text.


BLOCK 1B — HUMAN REVIEW STATEMENT (Platform Version)
For Fiverr/Upwork gig description or bio — add this line:

Every deliverable is reviewed by our team before it ships — including a former technical writer — to ensure clarity, accuracy, and usability.

(Note: "our team" = Phil + Debbie. Accurate and professional.)


BLOCK 1C — COPYRIGHT & OWNERSHIP (Platform Version)
For Fiverr FAQ — Question:

Who owns the prompts and outputs after delivery?

Answer:

You own everything I deliver to you. Once payment is complete, all prompts, documentation, and instructions are yours — use them, modify them, build on them however you like. I retain no rights to your deliverables after project completion.


BLOCK 1D — PROJECT MOMENTUM GUARANTEE (Platform Version)
(Formerly: No-Show / Non-Responsive Client Policy)

For Fiverr FAQ — Question:

How do you keep my project on track and on schedule?

Answer:

To ensure your custom prompt is tested and delivered at peak quality — without losing the specific details of your project — we work within rapid 48-hour communication windows. This keeps your build moving fast and ensures our AI models retain the exact context of your business. If you need to reschedule a call, one free reschedule with 2 hours notice is included. This structure protects your timeline, not just ours.


BLOCK 1E — REVISION POLICY (Platform Version)
For Fiverr FAQ — Question:

What is included in a revision?

Answer:

A revision is a refinement of the original deliverable based on your original requirements — your chance to fine-tune the result. Revisions are included per package: Basic = 1, Standard = 2, Premium = unlimited. If your goals shift after delivery, that becomes a new project, and I'll always let you know before any additional charges apply. I believe in being clear about scope so there are never any surprises.


SECTION 2 — DIRECT CLIENT CONTRACT BLOCKS
(Formal language. Use for off-platform clients — email agreements, signed contracts.)

BLOCK 2A — AI QUALITY COMMITMENT (Formal Version)
(Formerly: AI Accuracy Disclaimer)

AI Output Quality Commitment Prompt Works tests all prompts and reviews all deliverables prior to delivery. Client acknowledges that AI-generated content is probabilistic by nature and may occasionally produce unexpected results. Prompt Works includes usage instructions and evaluation guidance with every deliverable to help Client assess outputs confidently. Client is responsible for final review and approval of all AI-generated content prior to publication, distribution, or business use. Prompt Works makes no warranty regarding the accuracy or fitness for purpose of AI-generated output, but commits to delivering a tested, documented, and human-reviewed product.


BLOCK 2B — HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP COMMITMENT (Formal Version)
(Formerly: Human-in-the-Loop Mandate)

Human Review Commitment Every Prompt Works deliverable is reviewed by at least one qualified team member with a background in technical writing and business communications before delivery. This review confirms that deliverables meet Prompt Works' internal quality standards and are ready for client use. It does not constitute a guarantee of AI output accuracy, but ensures that every product leaving our team has been read, tested, and approved by a human being.


BLOCK 2C — COPYRIGHT & OWNERSHIP (Formal Version)
Intellectual Property and Ownership Upon receipt of full payment, Client receives full ownership of all deliverables including prompts, documentation, instructions, and any associated materials. Prompt Works retains no license, rights, or claims to client deliverables after project completion and payment. Prompt Works retains the right to reference the general nature of services provided (e.g., "AI workflow automation for a retail client") in portfolio descriptions without disclosing confidential client information. Client retains all rights to their own input data, business information, and pre-existing intellectual property provided to Prompt Works during the project.


BLOCK 2D — PROJECT MOMENTUM GUARANTEE (Formal Version)
(Formerly: No-Show / Missed Meeting Policy)

Project Momentum Guarantee To protect the integrity and timeline of your project, Prompt Works operates within structured 48-hour communication windows. This ensures that project context, AI model calibration, and deliverable quality are maintained throughout the engagement. Client may reschedule any scheduled call with a minimum of 2 hours notice at no charge, one time per project. Missed calls without prior notice forfeit that session. A rebooking fee equal to the original rate applies to schedule a replacement. If two or more scheduled sessions are missed within a single engagement without notice, Prompt Works reserves the right to close the project. These windows exist to keep your project moving — not to create barriers.


BLOCK 2E — CUSTOMIZATION FOCUS POLICY (Formal Version)
(Formerly: Revision and Scope of Work)

Customization Focus Policy By adhering closely to the original project scope, Prompt Works ensures you receive a highly targeted, precisely tuned deliverable — not a bloated, generic one. Revisions are defined as refinements to the deliverable based on original stated requirements. Changes to goals, requirements, or scope after project commencement represent new work and will be quoted separately before any additional work begins. If new ideas emerge during the build, Prompt Works will catalog them for a Phase 2 expansion so they don't dilute the power of your current deliverable. Revision limits by package: Basic = 1, Standard = 2, Premium = unlimited. Revision requests must be submitted within 7 days of delivery.


SECTION 3 — THE CUSTOMIZATION FOCUS SCRIPT
(Formerly: The Scope Creep Script) (Use verbally or in messages when a client starts expanding the project.)

The 5-Step Professional Response:

Step 1 — Appreciate:

"That's a great idea — I can see exactly why you'd want that."

Step 2 — Protect the current build:

"To make sure your current prompt stays sharp and focused, I want to keep this build on its original track."

Step 3 — Offer the upgrade:

"I'd love to build that for you as a Phase 2 — it deserves its own focused attention."

Step 4 — Quote:

"Based on what you're describing, that would be [X] at my standard rate."

Step 5 — Close:

"Want me to set that up as a new order once this one ships, or would you like to start it now?"

WORD GUIDE
Simple Explanations for Confusing Terms

INCOME WORDS
❌ GROSS INCOME
✅ TOTAL EARNED
What it means: The full amount the client paid you. Before any fees or expenses.

Example: Client pays you $50. That's your TOTAL EARNED.


❌ NET INCOME
✅ WHAT YOU KEEP
What it means: The money that actually hits your bank account. After platform takes their cut.

Example: Client pays $50. Upwork takes $5 fee. You keep $45. That $45 = WHAT YOU KEEP.


TAX WORDS
❌ EXEMPTION
✅ (I won't use this word - not relevant to you)
What it means: Something that completely excuses you from a requirement.

Why you don't have exemptions: You have to report income. You have to pay taxes. No exemptions for gig work.


❌ DEDUCTION
✅ BUSINESS EXPENSE
What it means: Money you spent to do the work. Computer, internet, software. Reduces what you pay taxes on.

Example: You earned $500. You spent $100 on a laptop for work. IRS taxes you on $400 (not $500). That $100 = BUSINESS EXPENSE.


SSA WORDS
❌ SUBSTANTIAL GAINFUL ACTIVITY (SGA)
✅ THE $1,620 LIMIT
What it means: If you earn more than $1,620 in a month, SSA thinks you can work full-time. Could affect your benefits.

Why it matters: Stay under this limit. Your spreadsheet warns at $1,400.


❌ TRIAL WORK PERIOD (TWP)
✅ YOUR 9 TEST MONTHS
What it means: SSA gives you 9 months to "test" if you can work. Any month you earn over $1,110 counts as 1 test month. You keep full benefits during all 9 months.

Why it matters: You can earn $10,000 in a test month. Still get full SSDI benefits. SSA is just tracking it.


PLATFORM WORDS
PLATFORM
What it means: Where you found the client.

Examples:

Upwork
Fiverr
Freelancer
Direct (friend referred)


PAYMENT METHOD
What it means: How the money moved to your bank.

Examples:

PayPal
Venmo
Direct deposit
Check

Do you track this? NO - Not needed.


SPREADSHEET WORDS
TOTAL EARNED
What it means: Sum of all "What You Keep" for the month. The number you report to SSA.

Where to find it: Big blue number in spreadsheet. Says "TOTAL EARNED THIS MONTH"


MONEY TO SAVE FOR TAXES
What it means: About 30% of what you earned. Put this in savings immediately. You'll need it for quarterly tax payments.

Where to find it: Big red number in spreadsheet. Says "MONEY TO SAVE FOR TAXES"


ROOM LEFT THIS MONTH
What it means: How much more you can earn before hitting $1,400. Your safety buffer.

Where to find it: Green number in spreadsheet. Says "Room Left This Month"

Example: You've earned $900 this month. Room left = $500. You can take projects up to $500 more.
